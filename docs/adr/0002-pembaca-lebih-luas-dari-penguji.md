# Pembaca is wider than the examiner

The Portofolio Deck was described as something the course examiner reads. The Mahasiswa wants anyone interested in them and their finished projects to be able to read it. The deck is for that Pembaca. The Penguji is one of them, not the only one. The deck still shows Proyek that are already built. It does not sell those projects as products, and it does not ask the Pembaca for a decision.

## Considered Options

- Examiner only. Rejected because the deck should also make sense to someone who is simply interested in the Mahasiswa.
- A pitch aimed at buyers, clients, or investors. Rejected because nothing is being sold.
